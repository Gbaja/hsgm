Full Logistics Capability

End-to-end logistics — from factory to field

We don't just source the equipment. We get it to you. HSGM manages the entire journey from manufacturer to project site, using direct relationships with airlines, shipping lines, and freight agents built over many years of business together — which means you get the best rates, not the best-available rate on a portal.

Warehousing

Short and long-term storage across our facilities in London, Nigeria, and Italy. We handle receipt, inspection, consolidation, and dispatch — your goods are safe and accounted for until you need them.

All Modes of Freight

Air for urgent or high-value cargo. Sea for bulk or heavy shipments. Road for regional delivery. We choose the right mode — or combine them — based on your deadline, budget, and cargo requirements.

All Incoterms Supported

EXW, FOB, CIF, DAP, DDP — whatever your trade terms, we structure procurement and logistics around them. No ambiguity, no arguments at the border.

Cargo Insurance

We arrange comprehensive cover on every shipment — your goods are fully protected from the moment they leave the supplier to the moment they arrive with you. Loss, damage, and delay are all covered.

Customs & Compliance

We manage import/export documentation, duties, and regulatory requirements across all jurisdictions — including specialist handling for controlled equipment entering oil and gas markets in Nigeria, Italy, and beyond.

Best-Price Freight Rates

We go directly to the airlines, shipping lines, and agents we've worked with for years — not a booking portal. That history means better rates, better service, and a direct line when something needs to move fast.

Who We Are

We source things. For anyone. Anywhere.

We're not a large corporation with layers of account managers. We're a tight team of experienced procurement professionals who've spent over 12 years doing this — and who pick up the phone.

Our roots are in oil and gas, and that's still where much of our work comes from. We know valves inside out — the specs, the standards, the suppliers who actually deliver. But over the years, clients from completely different sectors have come to us with completely different requirements — and we've delivered. Construction materials, electrical components, industrial equipment, specialist parts. If it exists and can be sourced, we can find it, price it, and get it to you.

With offices and warehouses in London, Nigeria, and Italy, and trusted agents in China, the United States, and beyond, we have the physical presence to source from any manufacturer in any market — and the logistics network to deliver to your site wherever it is in the world. And through 12 years of direct relationships with OEMs like TAI Milano, Valpress, Bonomi Group, ABB, Swagelok, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Eaton, and many more, we bring you factory-level pricing and genuine technical support — not just a catalogue price.
